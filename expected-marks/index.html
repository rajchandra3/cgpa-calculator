Frequently Asked Questions

How are VIT Theory internals capped and weighted?

Theory internals equal min((CAT 1 + CAT 2) × 0.5 + Internals/DA + Additional Learning, 60), plus FAT × 0.4 out of 100.

How are Lab and J-Component weighted when included?

When both Lab and J-Component are entered, the final score weights Theory at 60%, Lab at 20%, and J-Component at 20%. When only one is entered, Theory is weighted at 75% and that component at 25%.

What if I want to skip Lab or J-Component for a theory-only subject?

Leave the optional Lab and J-Component fields blank. If you enter 0 in either optional field, the calculator intentionally treats it as a 0% component score.
