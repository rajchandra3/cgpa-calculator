Frequently Asked Questions

What grade scale does the Anna University CGPA calculator use?

This calculator uses Anna University's 10-point credit grading system: O = 10, A+ = 9, A = 8, B+ = 7, B = 6, C = 5, and U / RA (Re-appearance) = 0. Semester GPA is the credit-weighted average of grade points across all courses.

How is Anna University CGPA calculated from semester GPAs?

Use the Quick CGPA tab if you already know your cumulative CGPA and credits up to the previous semester, or use All-Sem CGPA to enter each semester's GPA and total credits. CGPA equals the sum of (semester GPA × semester credits) divided by total cumulative credits.

How are U or RA arrears handled in Anna University GPA?

Courses with a U or RA grade carry 0 grade points while their credits are included in the semester denominator when selected. Once you clear the paper in a re-examination, recalculate using your updated passing grade.

How long has this CGPA calculator been available for students?

Launched in 2017 and backed by over 1M+ views since 2017, this platform is trusted by VIT & engineering students across India for over 9+ years. All calculations happen privately in your browser with offline support.
