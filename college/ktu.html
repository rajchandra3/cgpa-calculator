Frequently Asked Questions

What grade points does KTU (APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University) use?

KTU uses a 10-point scale with half-point steps: S / O = 10, A+ = 9, A = 8.5, B+ = 8, B = 7.5, C+ = 7, C = 6.5, D = 6, P = 5.5, and F / FE / I = 0. All ten grade options are built directly into the Semester GPA tab.

How do I calculate KTU SGPA and CGPA?

Select the grade and credit value (1 to 6 credits) for each subject in the Semester GPA tab to compute your SGPA. To compute cumulative CGPA across semesters, use either Quick CGPA with your prior credits and CGPA, or All-Sem CGPA to enter every semester individually.

How do I convert KTU CGPA to percentage?

A common approximation displayed alongside your score is CGPA × 10, while KTU's official conversion formula for certain regulations is (CGPA × 10) - 3.75 or as notified by APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University for your admission scheme.
