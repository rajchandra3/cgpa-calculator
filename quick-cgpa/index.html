Frequently Asked Questions

How is updated CGPA calculated in Quick CGPA?

Updated CGPA equals ((Prior CGPA × Prior Credits) + (Semester GPA × Semester Credits)) divided by (Prior Credits + Semester Credits), rounded to two decimal places.

Can I carry my Semester GPA directly into Quick CGPA?

Yes. After computing your Semester GPA, click the Check updated CGPA link under your result to automatically carry forward your semester GPA and total credits.

What should I enter if this is my very first semester?

Enter 0 for completed credits and 0 for prior CGPA, then enter your current semester credits and GPA. Your updated CGPA will equal your first-semester GPA.
