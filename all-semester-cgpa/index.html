Frequently Asked Questions

How is All-Semester CGPA calculated?

Cumulative CGPA is the credit-weighted average across all entered semesters: sum(Semester GPA × Semester Credits) divided by sum(Semester Credits), rounded to two decimal places.

Do I need to fill all 8 default semester rows?

No. Blank semester rows are skipped automatically, and you can add up to 12 semesters using the Add Semester button.

When should I use All-Sem CGPA instead of Quick CGPA?

Use All-Sem CGPA to compute your cumulative CGPA from scratch across individual semesters, or use Quick CGPA when you already know your previous cumulative CGPA and credits.
