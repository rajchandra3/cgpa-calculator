Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Target CGPA Planner compute required GPA?

Required GPA equals ((Target CGPA × (Completed + Remaining Credits)) − (Current CGPA × Completed Credits)) divided by Remaining Credits.

What does it mean when required GPA is above 10.00?

If the required GPA exceeds 10.00, that target cannot be mathematically reached with the remaining credits entered even if you score 10.00 in every future course. The planner also shows your maximum achievable CGPA.

Can I enter remaining credits across multiple future semesters?

Yes. Enter total remaining credits before graduation to get the overall average GPA needed, or enter just one upcoming semester's credits (such as 22) to check what you need next semester.
