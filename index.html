Frequently Asked Questions

How is VIT GPA calculated under the FFCS system?

Each course grade is converted to grade points on the VIT 10-point scale (S = 10, A = 9, B = 8, C = 7, D = 6, E = 5, F/N = 0). Your semester GPA equals the sum of (grade points × course credits) divided by total registered credits, rounded to two decimal places.

How does the VIT Expected Marks calculator handle Theory, Lab, and J-Component?

Theory internals are computed as min((CAT 1 + CAT 2) × 0.5 + Internals/DA + Additional Learning, 60), plus FAT × 0.4. If both Lab and J-Component are entered, the final score weights Theory at 60%, Lab at 20%, and J-Component at 20%. If only Lab or only J-Component is entered, Theory is weighted at 75% and that component at 25%. Leave both blank for theory-only courses.

Why do VIT students trust this CGPA calculator?

Built in 2017 for VIT Vellore and Chennai students, this calculator has served over 1M+ views since 2017 and is trusted by VIT students for over 9+ years. It runs locally in your browser with zero ads, offline PWA support, and instant recalculation.

What is the difference between Quick CGPA and All-Semester CGPA?

Quick CGPA updates your existing cumulative CGPA using just your prior completed credits, prior CGPA, and current semester credits and GPA. All-Semester CGPA lets you enter individual semester GPAs and credits across 1 to 12+ semesters to compute your cumulative CGPA from scratch.
