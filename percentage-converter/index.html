Frequently Asked Questions

How does VIT convert CGPA to percentage?

VIT converts CGPA on its 10-point FFCS scale directly using CGPA × 10 (for example, 9.25 CGPA equals 92.50%).

What formula does KTU use for CGPA to percentage?

KTU's official regulation formula subtracts 3.75 after multiplying by 10: Percentage = (CGPA × 10) − 3.75, floored at 0%.

Can I convert percentage back to CGPA?

Yes. Switch the direction dropdown to Percentage → CGPA to convert any percentage score back onto the university's 10-point scale.
